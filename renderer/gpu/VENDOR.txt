The GPU layer (gpu/) is the port's own code: command processor (cp/), GCN hardware definitions
(gcn/), shader translator GCN -> SPIR-V (shader/), renderer (render/), buffer and texture caches
(buffers/, textures/), memory tracking (memory/), platform libraries (platform/). It started
from shadPS4 (https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4, commit
b4e7ae776071ceb1dd35004577a8719481e1d258, GPL-2.0-or-later) and was rewritten part by part;
gpu/shadps4 was removed on 2026-10-13. The shader translator was checked against the shadPS4
translator it replaced on a corpus of the game's shaders (byte-identical SPIR-V,
tests/test_shader_corpus.cpp).

The upstream online module (gpu/bbnet) was removed in skyrender; see NOTICE.md.

Third-party libraries in third_party/: sirit (SPIR-V emitter), gcn (AMD GCN headers), half,
httplib, imgui, fonts. The upstream temporal upscalers (upscale/, the DLSS bridge, amd-fsr1 and
fsr-vulkan) were removed in skyrender; see NOTICE.md.
